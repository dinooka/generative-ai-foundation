### Question 16

1. AI-generated content that manipulates videos or images to misrepresent individuals → **Deepfake**
2. A misleading or false statement that harms a person or business's reputation → **Defamation**
3. A type of copyright that allows creators to share their work under specific conditions → **Creative Commons License**
4. Laws protecting the personal data of EU citizens → **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**
5. A form of legal protection that covers creative works like books, music, and software → **Intellectual Property (IP)**

### Question 17

1. AI reflects only perspectives that align with certain beliefs, reinforcing pre-existing opinions → **Confirmation Bias**
2. Built-in safety measures that help AI avoid generating harmful or inappropriate content → **Guardrails**
3. When AI favors or underrepresents certain groups due to skewed training data → **Representation Bias**
4. The way a user's question or phrasing influences AI's response, sometimes unintentionally introducing bias → **Prompt-Induced Bias**
5. AI assumes something is true based on patterns it has learned, even if those patterns contain unconscious assumptions → **Implicit Bias**

### Question 18

1. A process where AI teams determine how their model may respond to various inputs, cultural contexts, and ethical dilemmas → **Scenario-testing**
2. A process where external perspectives are used to evaluate AI fairness and accuracy → **Community Auditing**
3. The involvement of people in reviewing AI decisions to provide ethical reasoning and context → **Human Oversight**
4. The ability to critically analyze and evaluate information from various media sources → **Media Literacy**
5. The challenge of ensuring AI systems do not act in ways beyond human control → **Containment Problem**
