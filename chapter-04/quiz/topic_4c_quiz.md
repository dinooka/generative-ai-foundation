### Question 10

1. A process where AI teams determine how their model may respond to various inputs, cultural contexts, and ethical dilemmas → **Scenario-testing**
2. The involvement of people in reviewing AI decisions to provide ethical reasoning and context → **Human Oversight**
3. The ability to critically analyze and evaluate information from various media sources → **Media Literacy**
4. A process where external perspectives are used to evaluate AI fairness and accuracy → **Community Auditing**
5. The challenge of ensuring AI systems do not act in ways beyond human control → **Containment Problem**
