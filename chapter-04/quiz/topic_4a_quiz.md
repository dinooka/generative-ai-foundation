### Question 8

1. The tendency to accept only information that supports existing beliefs → **Confirmation Bias**
2. Making assumptions about humans based on incomplete data → **Stereotyping**
3. Biases we may not be aware of that exist in ourselves → **Implicit Bias**
4. Inability to accurately portray different groups due to limited data → **Representation Bias**

### Question 10

1. AI reflects only perspectives that align with certain beliefs, reinforcing pre-existing opinions → **Confirmation Bias**
2. AI assumes something is true based on patterns it has learned, even if those patterns contain unconscious assumptions → **Implicit Bias**
3. When AI favors or underrepresents certain groups due to skewed training data → **Representation Bias**
4. Built-in safety measures that help AI avoid generating harmful or inappropriate content → **Guardrails**
5. The way a user's question or phrasing influences AI's response, sometimes unintentionally introducing bias → **Prompt-Induced Bias**
