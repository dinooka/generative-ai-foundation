### Question 11

1. A type of copyright that allows creators to share their work under specific conditions → **Creative Commons License**
2. A form of legal protection that covers creative works like books, music, and software → **Intellectual Property (IP)**
3. AI-generated content that manipulates videos or images to misrepresent individuals → **Deepfake**
4. Laws protecting the personal data of EU citizens → **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**
5. A misleading or false statement that harms a person or business's reputation → **Defamation**
