# Topic 4A: Identifying and Mitigating Biases

> Study notes summarized in our own words. Not verbatim course text. Single scrollable page.

## Key points
- AI mirrors its training data: models absorb the biases embedded in what they were trained on.
- Bias categories: implicit bias, confirmation bias, stereotyping, and representation bias; plus gender, racial, disability, age, cultural/religious, language/nationality, and economic-status biases — visible across text, images, and video outputs.
- Model-specific biases: differently trained models can carry different biases; know each tool's strengths and weaknesses.
- Creator-introduced bias: **guardrails** (built-in rules blocking harmful content) reflect the creators' own perspectives and can exclude viewpoints; keyword filtering, content moderation, and cultural-sensitivity filters each trade protection against over-blocking useful or diverse content.
- Prompt-induced bias: leading questions prime slanted answers; mitigation includes neutral phrasing and explicitly requesting counterarguments.
- Bias manifests as stereotypes, discriminatory language, and unequal representation.
- Propagation risk: generative AI can spread bias at scale through mass-produced outputs.
- Embedded knowledge checks appear after sections.

bias, implicit bias, confirmation bias, stereotyping, representation bias, guardrails, prompt-induced bias, content manipulation.
