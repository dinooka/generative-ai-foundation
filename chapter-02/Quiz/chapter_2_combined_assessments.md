### Question 2

1. A numerical value that ensures consistency across multiple image generations → **Seed Number**
2. A setting that determines the shape and dimensions of an image → **Aspect Ratio**
3. A setting that adjusts the focus of an image to emphasize certain elements → **Depth of Field**
4. Descriptive text that provides context for visually impaired users or improves search engine visibility → **Alt Text**
5. A prompt parameter that controls the overall artistic style of an AI-generated image → **Style Prompts**

### Question 15

1. The technique of adding realistic motion effects to still images for a more immersive experience → **Subtle Animation**
2. The use of AI-generated characters to present or narrate video content → **Virtual Avatars**
3. The enhancement of black-and-white footage by adding appropriate color schemes → **Colorization**
4. The process of displaying synchronized text for improved video accessibility → **Subtitling**
5. The process of generating smooth transitions between frames for a cohesive video sequence → **Interpolation**

### Question 16

1. The phenomenon where AI models develop unexpected abilities beyond their training → **Emergence**
2. The process of breaking text into smaller units to help AI process and generate language → **Tokenization**
3. The act of condensing information into a shorter, more digestible form → **Summarization**
4. Setting specific boundaries to guide AI-generated content in a unique direction → **Creative Constraints**
5. Prompting AI to retrieve specific facts, statistics, or explanations → **Content Gathering**
