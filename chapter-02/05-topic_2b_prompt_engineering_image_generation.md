## Elements of an Image Prompt

A good image prompt gives the AI model context surrounding these areas:

- **Content Details:** Specify objects, characters, and scenes.
- **Style Choice:** Indicate artistic style (e.g., watercolor, comic book, realistic).
- **Color Preferences:** Mention desired color schemes.
- **Emotion or Mood:** Set the atmosphere or emotional tone.
- **Composition Tips:** Guide the arrangement of elements.
- **Perspective Clues:** Specify viewpoints or camera angles.
- **Scene Setting:** Define the location and time of day.
- **Unique Elements:** Request specific or unusual details for a creative touch.

## Using AI to Produce an Image for a Specific Purpose

Some examples of image prompts tailored to different end goals are as follows:

- **Marketing Materials:** "Create a swimwear ad with a diverse group of friends enjoying a sunny beach day together. Use a vibrant rainbow palette, emphasizing blue and yellow tones."
- **Illustrations:** "Illustrate a young girl exploring a magical forest with glowing mushrooms and a unicorn. Use a whimsical fairytale art style."
- **Concept Art:** "Design a futuristic cityscape at night with neon lights, flying cars, and skyscrapers in a cyberpunk style with dark blues and neon pinks."

## Techniques for Refining Image Prompts

Like any typical text prompt, an AI model will unlikely produce a perfect output upon its first generation. Users may also need to consider the following actions or skills:

- **Iterations:** Ask the AI model to produce additional variations.
  - Example: "Try again with darker colors."
- **Use References:** Mention specific styles, genres, or artists.
  - Example: "In the style of a 1950s comic book."
- **Combine Elements:** Mix different ideas for unique results.
  - Example: "A thundercloud and a girl with a red umbrella dancing in the rain."
- **Request Unique Elements:** Ask for unexpected or creative features.
  - Example: "Generate a library scene with a mythical creature."
- **Organize Layout:** Specify how to arrange the elements.
  - Example: "Center the main character with detailed background elements."
- **Use Multiple Tools:** Combine different AI tools for better quality.
  - Example: Use one tool to generate an image and another to refine text or elements, like Canva's Magic Tool.

## Advanced Image Prompting Techniques

Once users are familiar with the more fundamental aspects of image generation, they can begin experimenting with more advanced techniques.

- **Aspect Ratio (–AR):** Controls image shape and size.
  - Example: "Create a mountain landscape –AR 16:9."
- **Seed Number (–seed):** Produces more consistent variations or quality.
  - Example: "Generate a city skyline –seed 1234."
- **Style Prompts (–style):** Directs the overall artistic style.
  - Example: "Create a knight's portrait –style Renaissance painting."
- **Resolution (–res):** Sets the image's clarity and level of detail.
  - Example: "Starry night sky –res 1024×1024."
- **Depth of Field (–DOF):** Adjusts the focus of the image.
  - Example: "Flower with shallow depth of field –DOF shallow."

## Transforming an Image

AI can transform existing images by applying different artistic styles, adding or removing objects, tweaking small details, or completely altering the mood and atmosphere.

- Example: "Turn this city skyline into a Van Gogh-style painting with swirling skies."
- Example: "Replace the soccer ball with a beach ball, adjusting shadows to match."
- Example: "Change this garden photo into a moonlit scene with glowing fireflies."

Of course, using a small creator or artist's style without permission is an example of an unethical use case here.

## Images and Accessibility

Users can also prompt AI models to provide descriptions of images for visually impaired users, commonly referred to as alt text. This improves accessibility and the search engine visibility of online content.

- Example: "Provide alt text for this classroom scene, including desk layout, number of students, and the visual cues on the whiteboard."
- Example: "Describe the emotions and body language in this family picnic photo."
- Example: "Generate a whimsical description of this park scene. Focus just on the most playful elements."
