# Answer Key

## Question 1

**Correct answer:** An entity defines what is and is not harmful content.

Guardrails are meant to protect people from harmful content. Thus, the bias comes from a potential overreach due to what an entity defines as harmful content, which could skew data within a model. Guardrails do not cause bias from prompts people use to get content. Guardrails are not a limitation to the amount of data stored in an AI model. Guardrails do not cause bias if they are turned off.

## Question 2

**Correct answers:** Age; Nationality

Age and nationality can be a springboard for bias in data for a model used in Generative AI. Technological ability is not a source of bias for data. Incomplete data is not a source for bias for data.

## ⭐ Question 3

**Correct answers:** Unusual negative sentiments due to an event; Lack of diversity.

Lack of diversity in data and unusual negative sentiments due to an event can cause bias within a dataset. Lack of data does not necessarily cause bias. Anomalies in data do not directly cause bias.

## Question 4

**Correct answer:** Show me all the benefits of MySQL for storing physical fitness data.

This prompt can introduce bias because there is no balance of any other storage system and the negatives of using MySQL are not introduced. This situation may be done on purpose, but it still could propagate bias. The RDBMS prompt does not introduce bias because at least multiple options will be presented. The pros and cons prompt is a balanced prompt with no bias. The size limitation prompt is looking for a metric, so it's not a biased prompt.

## ⭐ Question 5

1. Generative AI can be used to propagate bias. → **Yes**
2. Not accounting for religious holidays when building a schedule is an example of bias. → **Yes**
3. Economic status can be a source for bias. → **Yes**

All three statements are true.

## ⭐ Question 6

**Correct answers:** Fine-tuning; Preprocessing techniques.

Fine-tuning and preprocessing techniques can cause different models to have different biases as the methods used for fine-tuning and preprocessing differ among models. Models that use shared training data tend to have the same biases. Tokenization does not usually lead to models having different biases. If anything, tokenization reduces biases.

## ⭐ Question 7

**Correct answers:** Race; Gender

Biases in generative AI are centered around personal biases, such as a person's gender or race. Location on its own does not cause bias. Statistical data on its own is not a source for bias.

## ⭐ Question 8

1. Copyrighted data is often used to train AI models. → **Yes**
2. A company's process should be documented and transparent when using AI in a professional environment. → **Yes**
3. A company should follow industry regulations regarding the use of copyrighted data in AI models. → **No**

There is no defined regulatory body for the use of copyrighted data in AI models. Companies should use non-AI practices and seek permission to use copyrighted data.

## Question 9

**Correct answers:** Copyright infringement; Claims of defamation.

Legal implications of using generated content inappropriately include claims of defamation and copyright infringement. Bias leads to possible inappropriate use of generated content. Tokenizing data removes personal information from data, so it would not lead to inappropriate use of generated data.

## Question 10

**Correct answer:** The company should seek permission to use the data.

The company should follow copyright laws and not use the data without the manufacturers' permission. There is no regulatory body or set of laws that drives how generative AI should be used. Implementing guardrails will not sift out copyright data. Changing the data could make it useless within the AI model.

## Question 11

**Correct answer:** Establish a policy disallowing data from being shared with public AI models.

The best the company can do is establish an internal policy preventing its data from going to specific places, including public AI models. There is no official regulatory body policy on how AI data should be handled. Not allowing AI tools to be used is not practical. Anonymizing data is done when data is entered into an AI model, not when people are typing prompts into a public AI model.

## Question 12

**Correct answers:** Company private data; Personal information

Personal information and company private data should not be part of training a Generative AI model. Historical data is often used to train an AI model. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are often used to train an AI model.

## Question 13

1. Human-generated content can be used to train an AI model. → **Yes**
2. People can opt out of having their data being used in an AI model. → **Yes**
3. Generative AI engines return the same personal data websites have on people. → **No**

Generative AI engines do not return personal data on people unless that data is entered into an AI model.

## Question 14

**Correct answers:** Civil; Criminal

Identity theft could result in civil and criminal actions taken against any entity that causes the identity theft. Identity theft does not inherently cause bias in data. Identity theft does not cause anonymization in data. Data should be anonymized to hide identities before an AI model is released for public use.

## Question 15

1. Static data masks permanently change data used in an AI model. → **Yes**
2. PII data left in Generative AI can cause identity theft. → **Yes**
3. Tokenization uses fictitious names to mask data. → **No**

Tokenization uses symbols to mask data. Pseudonymization uses fictitious names to mask data.

## Question 16

**Correct answers:** Bullying; Stalking

Bullying, hate crimes, fraud, stalking, and cheating can all lead to civil or criminal actions. Negative sentiments do not lead to civil or criminal actions. Exclusion and gossip, though potentially harmful, do not lead to civil or criminal actions.

## Question 17

**Correct answer:** Deepfake

A deepfake involves creating a realistic caricature of a person and then having the caricature say or do something it would not ordinarily do. A voice clone is a deepfake minus the image. A face swap is an image in which a face replaces another face on a body. A deepfake is a form of synthetic media, which is an image, audio, or video made to look realistic when it is not.

## Question 18

1. The first blank → **human oversight**
2. The second blank → **legal**

In a company, human oversight is needed to avoid spreading incorrect information. Doing so will help a company avoid financial and legal repercussions.

## Question 19

1. There is a fear that AI will take over jobs and humanity. → **Yes**
2. AI can serve as a job creator. → **Yes**
3. Every job will eventually be run by AI. → **No**

It is not yet known if most or all jobs will eventually be run by AI. The only certainty is that more jobs will use AI in some form or fashion.

## Question 20

1. Generative AI helps people learn more effectively. → **Yes**
2. Generative AI helps people do their jobs more efficiently. → **Yes**
3. Generative AI can reduce human interaction. → **No**

A reduction in human interaction is not considered to be a positive impact of Generative AI. Rather, that result can have a negative impact on society.

## Question 21

**Correct answers:** Generating a grocery list from a recipe; Generating a summary of large amounts of text.

Generative AI can help with life tasks such as generating a grocery list from a recipe or generating a summary of large amounts of text. Generative AI does not help create original content, such as a creative arts project, as the results always come from existing data. Generative AI is also not meant to help with emotional support.

## ⭐ Question 22

**Correct answers:** Real-time translation; Language learning lessons.

Generative AI provides real-time translation and language learning lessons, helping people communicate across languages more effectively. Grammar and style enhancements are done for same-language texts, so those features, while they help people communicate, do not necessarily help people communicate across languages.

## ⭐ Question 23

1. Generative AI risks reducing human interaction. → **Yes**
2. Generative AI can impact human motivation. → **Yes**
3. Generative AI replaces human contact. → **No**

Generative AI does not replace human contact.

## ⭐ Question 24

**Correct answers:** AI is equally available to all; AI will replace human contact.

People think AI may replace human contact, but it does not replace human contact. AI is not equally available to all, as many people do not have access to the technology needed for AI. AI will change the job landscape but will not eliminate every job. AI does analyze patterns and presents them as opportunities.

## Question 25

**Correct answer:** Due to increasing complexity, scale, and ambiguity in both AI systems and human values themselves.
