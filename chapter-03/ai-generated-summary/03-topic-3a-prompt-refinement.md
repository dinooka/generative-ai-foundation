# Topic 3A: Prompt Refinement for Targeted Outputs

> Study notes summarized in our own words. Not verbatim course text. Single scrollable page.

## Key points
- First outputs are rarely perfect: refinement is an iterative process requiring patience; specificity and clarity drive better targeting.
- **Specificity scale:** too-broad prompts leave the AI guessing (e.g., "write a short story" with no direction); too-narrow prompts overload with detail and confuse the model; a balanced prompt gives focused guidance plus creative room.
- **Context:** supply background the model cannot infer — audience, purpose, and desired outcome (e.g., volcano description tuned for age group; WWII narrative from a chosen perspective; fraction examples suited to young learners).
- **Clarity:** say exactly what you mean — vague verbs invite misreading; specify the process, the data type, and the reading level you want.
- **Assumptions:** never assume the model knows which "theorem," "plan," or cultural reference you mean — name people, events, and sources explicitly.
- **Style:** state the desired tone and format; style guides keep voice consistent across outputs.
- **Persona:** assigning the AI a role (e.g., friendly teacher) shapes how it answers.
- **Iterative prompting loop:** test the prompt, evaluate the output, refine the prompt, repeat.
