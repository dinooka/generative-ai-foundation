# Chapter 3: Vocabulary

> Study notes summarized in our own words. Not verbatim course text.
> The in-course item is an interactive H5P flashcard deck whose card text is not exposed; the terms below are the chapter's official Key Terms, defined in our own words.

- **Content Refinement** — improving AI-generated content through iterative edits.
- **Specificity** — the level of detail and precision in a prompt.
- **Prompt Balancing** — tuning a prompt to be neither too broad nor too narrow.
- **Context Clues** — background hints that guide the model's interpretation.
- **AI Conversation Flow** — the natural progression of a multi-turn chat with the model.
- **Unambiguous Prompts** — instructions written so only one interpretation is likely.
- **Context Specification** — explicitly stating the background the model should use.
- **Task Clarification** — spelling out exactly what the model must deliver.
- **Assumption Avoidance** — not presuming the model knows unstated references.
- **Persona Refinement** — tuning the role/voice the AI adopts.
- **Role-Based Prompting** — assigning the AI a persona (teacher, expert) to shape answers.
- **Context Refinement** — sharpening the background information supplied to the model.
- **Few-Shot Prompting** — giving a few examples so the model learns the pattern.
- **Pattern Recognition** — the model detecting structure/style from examples.
- **Model Learning** — how the model adapts its output based on prompt examples.
- **Glossary for Translation** — a term list supplied to keep translations consistent.
- **Domain-Specific Content** — material tailored to a particular field's terminology.
- **Templates** — pre-structured formats the model fills in.
- **Context Grounding** — anchoring the model in supplied documents/facts.
- **Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)** — combining retrieved documents with generation for grounded answers.
- **Conversation Memory** — the model's use of earlier turns in the same thread.
- **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning** — prompting step-by-step logical reasoning.
- **Zero-Shot Prompting** — asking without providing any examples.
- **Self-Consistency** — techniques that keep style, facts, and tone steady across outputs.
- **Generated Knowledge Prompting** — gathering facts first, then synthesizing them.
- **Prompt Chaining** — linking prompts so each output feeds the next step.
- **Reverse Prompting** — instructing the model what to exclude or avoid.
- **Content Control** — restricting what the output may contain.
- **Tone and Style Management** — directing the voice and manner of the output.
- **Fact-Checking** — verifying AI claims against reliable sources.
- **Cross-Referencing** — comparing claims across multiple sources.
- **Deductive Reasoning** — reasoning from general principles to specific conclusions.
- **Inductive Reasoning** — reasoning from specific observations to general conclusions.
