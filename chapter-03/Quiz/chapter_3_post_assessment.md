### Question 5

1. Asking AI to generate an output without any prior examples or context → **Zero-Shot Prompting**
2. Guiding AI to reason through a problem step by step, ensuring logical consistency → **Chain-of-Thought Prompting**
3. Having AI gather relevant information before completing a task to build better context → **Generated Knowledge Prompting**
4. Providing AI with examples before asking it to generate content to improve accuracy → **Few-Shot Prompting**
5. Breaking down a complex task into a sequence of prompts to improve accuracy → **Prompt Chaining**
