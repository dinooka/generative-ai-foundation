### Question 5

1. Breaking down a complex task into a sequence of prompts to improve accuracy → **Prompt Chaining**
2. Having AI gather relevant information before completing a task to build better context → **Generated Knowledge Prompting**
3. Asking AI to generate an output without any prior examples or context → **Zero-Shot Prompting**
4. Guiding AI to reason through a problem step by step, ensuring logical consistency → **Chain-of-Thought Prompting**
5. Providing AI with examples before asking it to generate content to improve accuracy → **Few-Shot Prompting**

### Question 14

1. Checking the accuracy of facts, dates, and statistics in AI outputs → **Fact-Checking**
2. Guiding AI to avoid certain topics, styles, or details in its response → **Reverse Prompting**
3. Ensuring AI-generated content stays appropriate for a specific audience → **Content Control**
4. The process of verifying AI-generated information against multiple reputable sources → **Cross-Referencing**
5. Questioning surprising or unusual facts and verifying the context of quotes or statistics to ensure they are not taken out of context → **Skepticism**

### Question 15

1. The process of testing, evaluating, refining, and repeating prompts to improve AI-generated outputs → **Iterative Prompting**
2. Information that is implied but not explicitly stated, which can lead to AI misinterpretation → **Assumptions**
3. Ensuring a prompt provides enough detail without being overly restrictive → **Specificity**
4. The precision of language used in a prompt to reduce vagueness and ambiguity → **Clarity**
5. The background information and details that help guide AI responses toward a relevant and appropriate output → **Context**
