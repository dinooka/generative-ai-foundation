### Question 13

1. The principle that computing power doubles approximately every two years → **Moore's Law**
2. The tendency of AI models to produce false but believable information → **Hallucinations**
3. The practice of questioning and evaluating AI-generated content instead of accepting it at face value → **Critical Thinking**
4. The way AI models reflect human assumptions and cultural perspectives in their outputs → **Bias**
5. The availability of AI tools to different users, depending on infrastructure and resources → **Accessibility**

### Question 14

1. Adjusting AI models to better align with user needs and preferences → **Customization**
2. The instructions given to an AI model to generate content → **Inputs**
3. The process of fine-tuning AI-generated content for improved accuracy and coherence → **Refinement**
4. The final product generated by an AI model based on given instructions → **Outputs**
5. The process of using multiple types of AI models or inputs to create more advanced content → **Multimodal Generation**

### Question 15

1. Processes that fine-tune AI-generated content for better accuracy, coherence, and relevance → **Refinement Algorithms**
2. A category of models that process and generate human-like language by understanding relationships between words → **Transformer Models**
3. The broad field of teaching machines to perform tasks that require human-like "thinking" → **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
4. A type of AI that creates new content, such as text, images, or music → **Generative AI**
5. A subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data and improve without explicit programming → **Machine Learning**
