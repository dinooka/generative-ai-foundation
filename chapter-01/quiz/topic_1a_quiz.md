### Question 8

1. **Predictive AI** forecasts future events by analyzing historical data.
2. **Discriminative AI** classifies data into categories, like facial recognition.
3. **Analytical AI** answers questions like "why" and "how" by examining complex datasets.
4. **Statistical AI** applies mathematical models to analyze data and make informed decisions.

### Question 9

1. Create realistic digital art and animations → **Diffusion Models**
2. Generate human faces or music tracks → **VAEs**
3. Generate hyper-realistic images and deepfake videos → **GANs**
4. Power image classification systems → **CNNs**

### Question 10

1. The broad field of teaching machines to perform tasks that require human-like "thinking" → **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
2. A subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data and improve without explicit programming → **Machine Learning**
3. A category of models that process and generate human-like language by understanding relationships between words → **Transformer Models**
4. Processes that fine-tune AI-generated content for better accuracy, coherence, and relevance → **Refinement Algorithms**
5. A type of AI that creates new content, such as text, images, or music → **Generative AI**
